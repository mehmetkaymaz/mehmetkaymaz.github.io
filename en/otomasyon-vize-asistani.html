Visa Application Assistant

A human-in-the-loop RPA system and dashboard that automates the repetitive parts of visa application forms and leaves critical steps to a person.

What it does

The system does the repetitive work; people make the decisions.

At consultancy firms, the same details are typed into forms and the same documents are uploaded again for every application. The assistant takes over this routine part. At steps that need a decision, such as login, verification codes and appointment selection, it stops and waits for the operator. When the operator clicks “Continue” on the dashboard, it picks up where it left off.

In short: Form filling and document uploads run automatically; at critical steps the system stops and waits for operator approval.

How it works

Step-by-step flow

  1. Application recordThe applicant's details are saved to a profile from the dashboard.
  2. SessionA persistent Chrome session is opened and the login step is left to the operator.
  3. Automated stepsIt navigates between pages, fills in form fields and uploads documents.
  4. Human approvalVerification codes, appointment selection and the final check are handled by the operator.

Highlights

What makes it strong

  • State machine

    Each step is a defined state (auto-fill, upload, validation, human approval, error, retry). After an error the flow resumes where it left off.

  • Data validation

    Form data and files are checked before they're submitted.

  • Controlled pace

    Rate limiting keeps requests at a steady pace.

  • Dashboard

    Adding applicants, starting the bot and approving pending steps all happen on one screen.

Architecture

Building blocks

  • Browserbrowser_manager

    Opens and manages the browser with a persistent session.

  • Automationnavigator

    Moves through the application pages step by step.

  • Automationform_filler

    Fills in forms with the applicant's saved details.

  • Automationfile_uploader

    Uploads the required documents to the right fields.

  • Checkvalidator

    Validates data before submission and stops records with errors.

  • Humanhuman_gate

    Stops at critical steps and waits for operator approval to continue.

  • Statestate_controller

    Tracks which stage each application is at and recovers from errors.

  • DashboardFlask dashboard

    Adding applicants, application statuses and activity logs.
