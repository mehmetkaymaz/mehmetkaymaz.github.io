Privacy and Cookie Notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Data controller

This notice is provided under Article 10 of Turkey’s Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) to inform visitors of the personal website at mehmetkaymaz.github.io. Data controller: Mehmet Kaymaz, İzmir, Turkey. Contact: mehmetkaymaz.web@gmail.com

2. What data is processed?

  • Contact details: The site has no form that collects or stores data. The contact form opens your message in your own email app. If you send it, your name, email address and message reach me by email. The same applies to any information you share when you call or email me.
  • Technical data: The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs such as IP addresses for security and operation. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your IP address and browser details are sent to Google.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Contact details are used only to reply to your message and to handle collaboration or job application processes, under KVKK Art. 5/2-c (directly related to entering into a contract) and Art. 5/2-f (legitimate interest). Technical data is processed under legitimate interest so that the site works securely and correctly. Your data is never used for advertising, marketing or profiling, and is never sold.

4. Transfers

Your personal data is not shared with third parties. Only the services the site relies on, GitHub (hosting), Google Fonts (fonts) and Gmail by Google (email), process technical data. Their servers are located outside Turkey, so data may be transferred abroad.

5. Cookies

This site does not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. When you dismiss the cookie notice, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage as mk-kvkk-bildirim-v1. It is never sent to a server and you can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

6. Your rights (KVKK Art. 11)

Under Article 11 of KVKK, you may contact the data controller to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed and, if so, request information about it,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected, and for it to be deleted or destroyed where the legal conditions are met,
  • ask for these actions to be notified to third parties the data was transferred to,
  • object to a result against you arising from analysis by automated systems,
  • claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

7. How to apply

Send your request by email to mehmetkaymaz.web@gmail.com. Requests are answered free of charge within 30 days at the latest.
